About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery opened in 2012 with one oven and a handful of family recipes. We named it after the star that guides travelers home, because we want every customer to feel at home here.

Our sourcing philosophy

We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from farms within an hour's drive, and fruit in season. Short supply chains mean fresher food and money that stays in our community.
